# Krassnigg Digital e.U.

**KI, die am Montag funktioniert.** — AI that works on Monday.

A one-person practice for AI and digital communication in Graz, Austria, founded and run by
[Andreas Krassnigg](https://github.com/akrassnigg) — physicist, former Head of AI and Data Science at Innophore,
Privatdozent at the University of Graz. We make AI usable for small and mid-sized businesses, associations and the
self-employed: AI days and workshops, AI consulting, AI video, and WordPress websites that are looked after.

### Open source

- **[Tooltips in a Nutshell](https://github.com/krassniggdigital/tooltips-in-a-nutshell)** — hovers, says its piece,
  gets out of the way. Shortcode tooltips for WordPress with no dependencies, and a drop-in for the `simple_tooltip`
  shortcode of *Simple Tooltips*, which wordpress.org closed in 2023 over an unpatched security issue.
  Background (German): [Simple Tooltips wurde geschlossen](https://websiteberater.com/simple-tooltips-ersatz/).
